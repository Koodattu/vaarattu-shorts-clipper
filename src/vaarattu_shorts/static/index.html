Mark a finished clip Ready for posting in the review queue and it appears here automatically. Approved clips still need that final check. You can publish ready clips while reviewing the rest of the recording.

Fill scheduled queue

Schedule unposted Ready for posting clips on your selected channels, one per day at a random time between 19:00 and 21:00 Finnish time (Europe/Helsinki), up to ten queued clips. Newest recordings come first; each recording's clips stay in source order. An unfinished recording group continues before the next one.
