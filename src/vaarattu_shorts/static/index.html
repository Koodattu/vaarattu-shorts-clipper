Daily posting plan

Choose finished recordings below. Clips stay grouped by recording, in their original order. New groups are appended after existing entries. The same video is intended for all three platforms.

Recording cleanup

Available after every clip is Not approved or Ready for posting. Preview the files and space first. Cleanup deletes source audio, downloaded sections, rejected renders and old revisions; it keeps final videos, captions and review records. Shared models stay available for future runs. Source editing will require processing the recording again.

Fill scheduled queue
